# Week 09 · Devlog

**Due:** Thu Oct 22, 2026 23:59 MT
**Points:** 5

## What to do

Commit `journey/journal/w09.md` to your repo with three sections:

```markdown
## Lecture: Space — The 3D Pipeline

**What landed.** One or two paragraphs on what actually changed your thinking.
Not a summary — a summary tells me you watched it, not that you thought about it.

**Where I'll use it.** Which part of your own project this touches, specifically.

**One thing I still don't get.** A real question. This one seeds Tuesday's discussion.
```

Then a short section recording any decision you made on your project this week and **why**. Six weeks from now you will not remember the reasoning, and that is the part that matters.

## How this is graded

**Push to your repository.** The autograder runs on the push and posts its
feedback as a **GitHub issue** on that repo, scored against the rubric below.
Read the issue; that is where your feedback lives.

There is nothing to submit in Canvas. Your commit history *is* the submission,
and the commit timestamp is what the late policy measures.

## Acceptance criteria

- All three lecture sections present and specific to this week's material.
- The open question is a real one, not "everything makes sense."
- At least one project decision recorded with its reasoning.
- Committed within the week it covers.
